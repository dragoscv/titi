First iPhone build of Titi.

Please try:
- Create a group, invite an Android or iPhone friend with the three words, the QR code or the link.
- Hold Talk to speak. Lock the phone and talk from the Push to Talk pill on the Lock Screen.
- Chat: reply, react, edit and delete (long-press a message).
- Long-press a member to block or report them.
- Join the demo group from the link in the App Review notes: the "Titi Demo" member replies, reacts and repeats your voice.

iPhone 0.1 talks over Bluetooth and the internet; same-Wi-Fi discovery comes in a later build.
